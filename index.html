Our workforce consulting framework follows a 3-pillar diagnostic and structural design approach, ensuring market competitiveness, internal equity, and financial sustainability[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span).

1

Getting Understanding[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span)

  • Obtain an understanding of overall business strategy, future objectives, financial goals, and rewards culture[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).
  • Review existing grading, compensation, benefits, and bonus structures through discussions with HR and key rewards personnel[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).
  • Conduct initial discussions on expected Rewards Philosophy, employee recognition, retention objectives, and future market positioning[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span).
2

Benchmarking Design, Data Collection & Validation[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span)

  • Conduct a desktop diagnostic review of base pay, allowances, bonuses, and core benefits[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span).
  • Benchmark existing rewards elements and reward mechanisms against industry best practices[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span).
  • Analyze compensation and allowances data (vehicle, retirement, fuel, etc.) to develop grade-wise compensation analysis and detailed benefits review[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span).
3

Rewards Structure and Report Development[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span)

  • Recommend and design an appropriate pay structure by analyzing market grades, midpoints, and percentiles[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span).
  • Develop aligned pay ranges and grading structures that define compensation limits and assess employee fitment to identify outliers[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span).
  • Present potential average cost-to-company and overall financial impact based on recommendations[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span).

Submit and review draft aligned with strategic objectives, incorporate feedback, and submit revised draft for finalization[span_16](start_span)[span_16](end_span).
